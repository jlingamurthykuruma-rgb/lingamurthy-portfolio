About Me

Hi, I'm Jangam Lingamurthy, an Actor and Oggu Katha Artist from Siddipet, Telangana. I am passionate about acting, storytelling, and bringing different characters and emotions to life.

My interests include acting, short films, theatre, and content creation. I have experience participating in short films, theatre performances, and YouTube projects, which have helped me develop my performance and camera skills.

I am passionate about growing as an artist and looking forward to bigger opportunities in films and the entertainment industry. I am always interested in creative projects where I can learn, perform, and showcase my talent.
